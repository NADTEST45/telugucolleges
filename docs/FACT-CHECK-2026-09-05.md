# Public information fact-check — 5 September 2026

This review inventories all 849 college records and all supporting public datasets. It is **not a certification that every fact is current**. The accompanying college coverage ledger explicitly marks fields not reverified. A working source link is not proof of the claims attributed to it.

## Confirmed corrections

- **TG 2025 final cutoffs:** independently downloaded and extracted all 33 pages of the [official final-phase statement](https://tgeapcetd.nic.in/files/TGEAPCET_2025_FINALPHASE_LASTRANKS.pdf). All previously stored numeric cells matched. Added 352 missing ranks across six self-finance codes (ESUTSF, JNPASF, JNTHMT, JNTSSF, KUEWSF, MGUNSF). After correction, **19,954 numeric cells, 951 branch rows and 163 source codes match**, with no mismatches. Preserve absent boys values at women-only colleges. Nine source codes have no master profile; their presence in a cutoff table does not establish a new college profile. Other TG years/phases and AP tables were not independently reconciled to fresh official PDFs.
- **Locations:** matched the official 2025 statement to 154 existing profiles; corrected **122 district fields**, many incorrectly labelled Hyderabad. District-code expansion preserves the site's existing district spellings. This validates the location in that statement, not later campus relocations, current affiliation or 2026 branch approval.
- **Rankings:** checked all 43 previously nonzero NIRF claims against the official [2025 Engineering top 100](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/EngineeringRanking.html), [101–150](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/EngineeringRanking150.html), [151–200](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/EngineeringRanking200.html), and [201–300](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/EngineeringRanking300.html). Aditya University belongs to 201–300, not 151–200. Removed unsupported 2025 Engineering badges for SRM AP, VIT-AP, Malla Reddy University and Vasavi. The lists do not substantiate their former values. Removed campus-specific badges for BITS Hyderabad, Amrita Amaravati, GITAM Hyderabad and KL Hyderabad; instead explain the parent institution's published ranking. Remaining 35 ranking values correspond to listed institutions. Band midpoint numbers are internal encodings, not exact ranks. Zero means no rank asserted in this dataset, not proof of non-participation. No 2026 rank was inferred.
- **Placement summaries:** the general NIRF ranking page does not substantiate median/average/highest salary or placed counts. Withdrew **14 detailed summaries** with only that citation; preserved their former contents in the audit archive. - **Invented courses/fees:** removed formula-generated M.Tech, MBA, MCA and MD/MS offerings and fees. A B.Tech fee does not establish that a college offers a postgraduate program. Removed inferred pharmacy fee multipliers and generic medical course generation; ambiguous prices display “Not verified”, not ₹0. Explicit university course records remain separate and require their own source checks.

Already-admitted engineering candidates are excluded from exercising options in this round. This is not an engineering extension.

Do not treat archived news or a global review date as verification of these matters.
